1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use GoalSats, we may collect and process the following information.

Account and authentication information

When you create or access a GoalSats account, we may process:

  • Email address
  • Firebase account/user ID
  • Username
  • Authentication information required to maintain your account
  • Information provided through supported sign-in providers, where applicable

GoalSats supports email/password authentication and Google sign-in. The application also contains Facebook authentication integration where that feature is enabled.

Authentication services may also process technical information such as IP addresses and user-agent information for authentication security, abuse prevention, and account management.

Profile information

You may provide information such as:

  • Username
  • Nationality or country selection
  • Profile picture/avatar
  • Profile and gameplay preferences

A profile picture may be stored as part of your GoalSats profile and may be displayed to other users in areas such as leaderboards, matches, or other multiplayer features.

Gameplay and account activity

GoalSats stores information necessary to operate the game and maintain your account, which may include:

  • Experience points and player level
  • Match statistics
  • Wins, draws, losses, and streak information
  • Leaderboard information
  • In-game SATP balances and related virtual-item information
  • Achievements, titles, badges, borders, boots, and other unlocked items
  • Tournament and match information
  • Rivalry and multiplayer information
  • Gameplay history
  • Referral information
  • In-game reward and transaction records
  • Contest participation, predictions, scores, rankings, and related contest statistics where applicable

SATP are virtual in-game points and do not represent cash, cryptocurrency, or a monetary deposit.

Push-notification information

If notifications are enabled, GoalSats may store a Firebase Cloud Messaging device token associated with your account so that we can deliver relevant app notifications.

You can control notification permissions through your Android device settings.

Device and technical information

GoalSats and the services used to operate the application may process technical information such as:

  • Device type and model
  • Android operating system information
  • Application and SDK information
  • IP address
  • User-agent information
  • Firebase application identifiers
  • Authentication and security information
  • Information necessary to detect abuse, protect accounts, and maintain service reliability

GoalSats does not request Android location permissions in the current application.

2. Advertising

GoalSats uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertising, including rewarded advertisements and banner advertisements.

Advertising providers may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising information, ad interaction information, and technical or diagnostic information as necessary to provide, measure, and protect advertising services.

GoalSats does not sell your personal information to advertisers.

Advertising providers operate according to their own privacy policies and applicable privacy controls.

3. How We Use Information

We use information for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing GoalSats accounts
  • Authenticating users
  • Providing game functionality
  • Maintaining player profiles
  • Operating matchmaking and multiplayer features
  • Maintaining leaderboards and rankings
  • Recording gameplay statistics and history
  • Maintaining virtual in-game balances and rewards
  • Processing in-game transactions and rewards
  • Operating contests and simulations
  • Verifying contest eligibility
  • Recording contest predictions and scores
  • Delivering push notifications
  • Preventing cheating, fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Protecting the security and integrity of the service
  • Troubleshooting and maintaining the application
  • Improving reliability and performance
  • Serving and measuring advertisements
  • Responding to support requests
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations

4. Contest Information

GoalSats may operate free-to-enter Football Simulation Contests.

Contest-related information may include:

  • Contest participation
  • Simulated-match predictions
  • Prediction results and scores
  • Contest rankings
  • Eligibility information
  • Contest statistics
  • Prize-related information where a user becomes an eligible winner

All GoalSats contest matches are simulated within GoalSats and are not real-world football fixtures.

Contest participation is subject to the applicable contest rules displayed in the application.

Where a physical prize requires delivery information from an eligible winner, GoalSats may request the information reasonably necessary to arrange delivery of that prize. Such information will be used for the applicable prize-fulfillment purpose.

5. Service Providers and Data Sharing

GoalSats uses third-party service providers to operate and maintain the application.

These may include:

Firebase and Google services

GoalSats uses Firebase services provided by Google, including services such as:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Firebase Cloud Functions
  • Firebase App Check
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Google Mobile Ads
  • Google Sign-In

These services may process information on our behalf or as necessary to provide their respective services.

Firebase states that data handled by applicable Firebase services is encrypted in transit and that Firebase provides mechanisms for developers to delete end-user data.

Social sign-in providers

If you choose to use a supported social sign-in method, the applicable provider may process information according to its own privacy policy.

For Google sign-in, Google may provide GoalSats with information required to authenticate your account, such as your email address and account identifier.

If Facebook sign-in is enabled and used, Facebook may process information associated with that authentication request according to Meta's applicable privacy practices.

We do not sell your personal information.

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide the service
  • Protect users and the platform
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, or cheating
  • Fulfill physical prizes
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Respond to valid legal requests
  • Protect the rights, property, or safety of GoalSats, our users, or others

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information associated with GoalSats accounts.

GoalSats uses Firebase authentication, authenticated backend services, Firestore security rules, server-side authorization, and other security controls to protect account and game data.

Data transmitted between the application and supported services is protected using encrypted network connections such as HTTPS/TLS where supported by those services.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, while we take reasonable measures to protect information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide GoalSats, maintain accounts, operate gameplay and contest features, maintain security, prevent fraud and abuse, resolve disputes, maintain legitimate game records, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required for these purposes, it should be deleted or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable data-protection requirements.

Some records may need to be retained for a limited period where necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. Where such retention is required, we will limit the retained information to what is reasonably necessary.

8. Account Deletion

GoalSats provides an in-app Delete Account option in Profile Settings.

Users may request deletion of their GoalSats account and associated personal data through the in-app account deletion feature.

Users may also contact GoalSats at:

Email Support: support@goalsats.com

When an account deletion request is successfully processed, GoalSats will delete the account and associated personal information from the applicable GoalSats systems, subject to any information that we are legally or legitimately required to retain for purposes such as security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

Deleting an account is different from simply logging out or disabling an account.

Deletion requests should clearly identify the GoalSats account concerned. We may take reasonable steps to verify the request before processing it in order to prevent unauthorized deletion.

Google Play requires apps that provide account creation to offer both an in-app deletion path and an external deletion resource.

9. Children's Privacy

GoalSats does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal information to us without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information from our systems.

The GoalSats Football Simulation Contest is restricted to users who meet the eligibility requirements stated in the applicable contest rules, including the stated 18+ requirement where applicable.
