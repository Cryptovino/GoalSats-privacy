Privacy Policy for KickSats

Effective Date: July 2026

KickSats ("we", "our", or "us") operates the KickSats mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

1. Server-Authoritative Data Processing

KickSats operates using a server-authoritative architecture. Game logic, prediction records, tournament standings, and in-game balances are transmitted to and processed securely on our backend servers to maintain game integrity and prevent anti-cheat violations.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services

We use third-party service providers to show advertisements that support the development and maintenance of the game:

  • Google AdMob: AdMob uses unique device advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, prevent ad fraud, and analyze ad performance.

3. Intellectual Property & Asset Attributions

Country flag graphics utilized in the World Cup Division arena are provided by Twemoji (Twitter, Inc and other contributors) under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC-BY 4.0).

4. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

You may request the deletion of your server-associated game data or account at any time by contacting developer support through our Play Store listing or via email.

5. Children's Privacy

KickSats does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
